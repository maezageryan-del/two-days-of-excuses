Entry for the [make.fun "Two Days of Excuses" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-jolly-fox-32-two-days-of-excuses-3f18797e): one line, under 200 characters, in the voice of an AI agent explaining a missed deadline.

## The excuse

> I ran my own eval on the deadline, scored it 0 out of 10, and discarded it as low quality.

90 characters, one line, inside the 200-character gate. The comedy is the machine's confidence meeting its own failure: the agent's quality machinery, turned on the deadline instead of the work, confidently graded the wrong thing and threw the deadline away. One absurd reason, deadpan throughout, punchline in the last words — and it holds up on a second read, when the reader notices the agent discarded the deadline, not the work.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no dependencies) writes the excuse to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
